Swift Rental · by The Anzy · coming soon

Hand over, take back and get paid.

Swift Rental runs a car or bike rental business from one app: every vehicle, every booking, and proof of its condition at handover and return.

Get early access

Built for rental owners and their staff

Everything from the booking to the final invoice, on one phone.

🪪

Scan the customer's ID

Scan an Aadhaar card or driving licence and the details fill themselves in. Only the last four Aadhaar digits are kept.

🎥

Handover video and photos

Record the vehicle from every angle before it leaves and when it comes back, so damage is never in dispute.

✍️

Customer signature

The customer signs on the phone to accept the vehicle's condition and your terms.

🔢

Odometer scan

Photograph the dashboard and the kilometres are read automatically; extra km are charged by your rules.

🧾

Bills and invoices

Rent, extra km, late return, fuel and damage worked out for you, shared as PDF or Excel.

📲

Customer link and QR

Customers see their booking and pay the balance by UPI from a link, with no app to install.

📈

Reports

Earnings, unpaid bills and how often each vehicle is out on rent, month by month.

👥

Staff accounts

Give each staff member their own login and switch it off when they leave.
